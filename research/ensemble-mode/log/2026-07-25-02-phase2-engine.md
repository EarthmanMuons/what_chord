# 2026-07-25: Phase 2 engine work and the implied-root ranking decisions

**Goal.** Land the ensemble-gated engine changes (ghost-root generation,
diatonic filter, missing-root pricing, bass neutralization, implied-root marker)
and record the design decisions the implementation forced beyond the plan's
sketch.

**Setup.** Engine work on top of the Phase 1 contract (`PlayingContext` on
`AnalysisContext`). No corpus measurement this entry; behavior verified by
hand-traced suite cases encoded as unit tests
(`packages/whatchord/test/ensemble_rootless_test.dart`).

**What happened.** The plan's pricing-only sketch is insufficient on its own,
for a reason the gate data already contained: a rootless reading must beat the
complete, zero-cost reading of the same tones (a rootless Cm9 is exactly a
0.00-cost Ebmaj7), and no positive missing-root price can do that. Three
mechanisms landed:

1. **Generation** (as planned): in ensemble context, absent key-diatonic pitch
   classes are hypothesized as implied roots against five ensemble-eligible
   templates (dominant7, major7, minor7, minorMajor7, halfDiminished7;
   diminished7 excluded as symmetric). Implied readings require every template
   tone (no missing-essential allowance) and reject any unexplained tone,
   mirroring the prototype's legality filter. Missing-root cost 0.25; bass
   placement cost waived (a guide tone in the lowest voice is the form, not an
   inversion signal).
2. **A hard ranking rule, not a price** ("prefer idiomatic implied-root
   reading", first in `hardRules`): implied-root candidates outrank
   sounding-root candidates outright. Implied candidates exist only under
   ensemble analysis, so the rule is structurally inert in solo mode, and
   placing it first keeps the sounding-root-era hard rules from ever deciding an
   implied-vs-sounding pair.
3. **An off-idiom exception** the corpus semantics do not rule on but common
   sense does: an implied reading carrying an altered extension on a
   non-dominant host (e.g. the Am7b9 ghost inside a played C7) does not get the
   hard preference and competes on cost, where it loses. Dominant hosts keep the
   full alt palette; #11 stays idiomatic on its Lydian-natural major-family
   hosts.

Pruning also had to become group-aware: implied and sounding candidates are
pruned against their own group's cheapest cost, since the sounding-root minimum
would otherwise evict the entire implied group before the hard rule could
promote it.

**Plain-English reading.** Turning ensemble mode on tells the app the root may
be in someone else's hands. The engine now takes that literally: whenever the
played notes spell a sensible rootless chord in the current key, that reading
wins, even though the same notes always also spell some complete chord. The
exception exists so that a chord you played complete, with its root, is not
re-explained as an exotic colored fragment of something else; only readings a
comping pianist would actually intend get the rootless privilege. A complete
plain voicing in ensemble mode can still be renamed (a played C-E-G can read Am7
rootless), which is the mode's contract per the corpus ruler, not a bug; the
on-screen mode indicator exists for exactly this reason.

**Decisions.**

- The implied-root preference is ranking policy (hard rule), not pricing, per
  the decision reasoning above. The plan text stands corrected by this entry.
- The off-idiom exception is adopted provisionally. It protects complete
  sounding chords and touches no suite or corpus-floor case by construction, but
  its effect at corpus scale is unmeasured until the Phase 3 harness runs; if
  the real-engine floor lands under the simulated 81.9%, this exception is the
  first suspect to re-examine.
- Solo invariance held with zero golden churn: solo output is bit-identical (all
  513 package and 227 root tests pass; the ghost loop, pricing branches, and
  hard rule are all structurally unreachable in solo analysis).
- The comping suite's behavioral encoding as package tests is deferred to Phase
  3 as planned; two of its cases (the F-B-E and F-A-B-E dominant-vs- tonic-stack
  shells) are expected to need the guide-tone/dominant-color tiebreak before
  they pass top-1.

**Next.**
