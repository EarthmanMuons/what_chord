**What happened.** `data/product-suite/suite-v0.json` now contains the exact
20-case inventory in its declared order:

- 3 inherited-source cases with 48 checkpoints;
- 12 authored musical-policy cases with 28 checkpoints; and
- 5 authored contract-mechanics cases with 32 checkpoints.

The 108 checkpoints retain literal construction, frame and onset-instance,
canonical candidate, candidate-bound cue, selector-stage, authorization, and
display expectations. Case-local candidate references resolve to exact ordered
root/quality identities and complete upper/lower MIDI assignments. The suite
includes a complete dominant seventh as an upper layer and as a lower layer, so
neither the data nor the scorer restores a triad-only upper assumption.

The first literal JSON expansion used a one-time, nonpersisted authoring helper
that applied the already-preregistered event, cue, selector-stage,
authorization, and display rules. It did not call a WhatChord product
implementation or inspect a product result. The helper was deliberately not
retained as an alternate selector oracle: the frozen JSON is the answer sheet,
the validator independently checks replay and candidate facts plus internal
stage/binding consistency, and the boundary tests make the highest-risk literal
values inspectable.

The validator rejects unknown fields, noncanonical JSON, changed dependency or
fixture digests, the wrong inherited roster or case order, unused fixtures,
nonconsecutive musical events, action time reversal, invalid reset placement,
changed replay frames, changed or reordered generated candidate lists, undefined
candidate references, invalid stage subsets, incomplete baseline references, and
missing reason or transition coverage. It independently replays frames and
regenerates structural candidates. Cue, raw-decision, authorization, and display
expectations remain literal suite data rather than being regenerated by the
validator.

The ruler contains 29 named-snapshot baseline targets and 20 adapted-stream
targets. Petrushka rehearsal 49 remains the sole ordered-composite snapshot
coverage exclusion; its 24 replay checkpoints still test structural abstention
and the other automatic dimensions.

`polychord-product-exact-scorer/1` imports no product selector or display
implementation. It requires the frozen suite digest, exact version IDs, every
checkpoint exactly once and in order, and exact equality in seven dimensions:
frame plus observation time, construction, candidates, cue records, raw
decision, authorization, and display. Coverage exclusions do not enter the
construction denominator and remain eligible in every other dimension.

The pinned scorer controls materialize one exact synthetic prediction and seven
single-dimension corruptions. The exact control passed:

```text
Stratum                        Frame and other dimensions   Construction
inherited-source                              48/48              24/24
authored-musical-policy                       28/28              28/28
authored-contract-mechanics                   32/32              32/32
suiteExactGatePass: true
```

Each corruption failed `suiteExactGatePass` and failed its targeted dimension:
frame, construction, candidate order, cue record, raw decision, authorization,
or display. Missing checkpoints and mismatched version IDs were rejected before
scoring. A separate control confirmed that changing a timer observation from 280
to 281 ms fails frame exactness even though its retained MIDI frame is
unchanged.

**Plain-English reading.** We now have a locked answer sheet and a grader before
writing the new detector. The answer sheet tests both musical-policy choices and
the state-machine behavior musicians would experience, including pedal,
reattack, reset, primary suppression, and exact timer boundaries. The grader can
recognize a perfect synthetic copy, detects one deliberately planted error in
every category, and cannot call the detector it will later grade.

**Decisions.** Freeze `polychord-product-suite/1` with `scoringAllowed: true`.
This enables scoring but does not count the exact synthetic copy as a product
result. Preserve the construction ruler, inherited replay manifest, and product
fixture manifest unchanged. Use case-local candidate references only as lossless
machine normalization; symbols remain presentation data.
