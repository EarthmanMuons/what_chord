What you will judge

In this study, a polychord is a useful constructional or notational description of two conventional chordal units in combination. It is not a claim that a listener hears two keys.

For each scored case, first judge the construction, then describe its layers, and finally say which input conditions contain enough evidence to recover your reading. You may always choose cannot determine and explain why.
