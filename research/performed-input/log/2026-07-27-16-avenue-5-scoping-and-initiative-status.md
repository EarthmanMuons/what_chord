# 2026-07-27: Avenue 5 scoped and shelved; initiative status

**Goal.** Scope avenue 5 (voicing-structure awareness) with the same
measurement-first discipline before any engine design: does the added-tone
family actually have the top-voice topology the melody-over-accompaniment
framing suggests?

**Setup.** Scratchpad topology census over A0 development added-tone
disagreement segments (same segment walk as the error census, method recorded
here): classify where the extra tones sit in the voicing (strictly above all
chord tones, strictly below, or interior), and measure the direct-mechanism
ceiling (would dropping the single top note leave exactly the analyst chord?).

**What happened.**

| extras position | share of added-tone time |
| --------------- | ------------------------ |
| topVoice        | 0.233                    |
| bassBelow       | 0.213                    |
| interior        | 0.554                    |

Dropping the top note yields exactly the analyst chord on 17.8% of added-tone
time (76.5% of the topVoice slice), which is about 1.2% of displayed time.

Verdict: the cheap, register-based version of voicing-structure awareness is
measured out. A "top voice" only exists in a quarter of the family; the majority
of extra tones sit inside the voicing (pedal-held harmony mixing registers,
accompaniment figures, inner ornaments) where no register rule can separate
them. The honest version of avenue 5 is polyphonic voice separation, tracking
melodic streams across events through time, a substantial research project whose
full prize on this ruler is the roughly 3.7% of displayed time in the heldDwell
bucket. Recorded and shelved: real, interesting, and priced.

**Plain-English reading.** The last engine idea was to teach the app that a
melody floating above a chord is not part of the chord. Measurement says the
floating-melody picture is true only a quarter of the time; the rest of the
stray notes live inside the chord's own registers, where telling them apart
means genuinely tracking voices through time, a big project for a small measured
prize. With that priced and shelved, the initiative's active research is done:
two rulers stand frozen with baselines, the naming gap is fully attributed, the
flicker finding is in UX's hands, and what remains in the queue is data work,
not engine work.

**Decisions.**

- Avenue 5 shelved with its ceiling measured; the README entry gains the
  verdict.
- The initiative stays open with avenues 3 and 4 queued and the test split held
  for a pre-declared confirmation spend.

**Next.** Avenue 4 (POP909 diligence) is the natural next opening when research

rulers, and its license and annotation-quality questions gate everything else
