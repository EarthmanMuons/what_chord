# 2026-07-28: Initiative start: the tolerance dial, both sides

**Goal.** Open the tone-pricing initiative: the engine-side descendant of
performed-input, taking up the two deliberately-shelved leads (superset
absorption, performed-input logs 2026-07-27-10/-11; shell omission, log
2026-07-28-05) as one explanation-cost tolerance question, sequenced before the
performed-input test-split spend so the holdout confirms the final engine state.

**Setup.** Scaffold only; sources are the performed-input logs cited throughout
README.md and PROTOCOL.md.

**What happened.** Initiative opened with the build order recorded in the
README: arm C simultaneity refinement first (BC is the residual-isolating
instrument and currently inflates the superset bucket with span-union
artifacts), then the baselines (the 8-plus pitch-class self-consistency census
reserved in performed-input log 2026-07-28-05, the post-refinement BC residual,
the standing-row inventory), then the experiment-mechanism scoping, then the
levers with pre-declared expectations.

Two protocol decisions from the opening review:

- **Review-on-flip replaces zero-flip for this initiative.** The reviewed oracle
  entries are justified snapshots of past engine behavior, not ground truth; an
  initiative whose purpose is moving naming thresholds treats flips as review
  triggers with exposure mass attached, never as automatic failures, and never
  silent. The zero-flip constraint stays in force outside this initiative.
- **The flagged high-mass soft verdicts become standing evaluation rows**,
  re-read against every candidate change rather than revisited on a calendar;
  any that flip re-review under the rule above.

**Plain-English reading.** The measurement program told us the engine's one
remaining live-input conversation is about tolerance: when an extra note should
be absorbed into a fancier name, and when a missing note should be forgiven at
an honest price. This initiative has that conversation with the instruments the
measurement program built, and the answer sheet from the old oracle reviews is
allowed to change, as long as every change is re-argued in writing.

**Next.** Arm C simultaneity refinement, then the baseline suite.
