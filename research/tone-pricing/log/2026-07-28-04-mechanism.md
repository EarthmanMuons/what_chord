# 2026-07-28: Experiment mechanism scoped and built; two blockers mapped

**Goal.** Build-order step 3: how pricing variants are prototyped without
touching shipped ranking.

**The map.** `ChordAnalysisProfile` switches tie-rule lists only (current versus
the frozen whatKeyPaper2026); the explanation-cost prices are constants in
`chord_analyzer.dart`. The superset lever's dial is `unexplainedToneCost`
(shipped 2.0): the price a base reading pays per sounding tone its name cannot
account for. The bass variant (`_bassUnexplainedCost`, 1.0) and the
color/vocabulary prices are separate constants, untouched for now.

**Two blockers mapped for the shell lever**, discovered at the cost site:

1. Power-quality candidates with any unexplained tone are rejected outright
   during pricing (`return null`), so a D5-plus-color reading of D-A-C cannot
   even enumerate. The shell lever is therefore not purely a price change; it
   needs either that gate relaxed for specific color intervals or new template
   forms.
2. The extension vocabulary has no seventh-as-color form (add(b7) or add(maj7)
   do not exist; sevenths are quality-defining), so the 5-based display forms
   the chord-symbols guide favors require vocabulary work regardless of pricing.

**Mechanism built.** `ChordAnalyzer` gains an `unexplainedToneCost` constructor
parameter defaulting to the shipped constant, documented as the tone-pricing
research override; profiles stay reserved for frozen reproducibility.
`replay_batch.dart` accepts `unexplainedToneCost` per request and builds a
dedicated analyzer when present; the extractor gains `--unexplained-tone-cost`,
suffixing the set name (-utc<value>) and recording the override in the manifest.
Package suite, root suite, and analyzers green; the app constructs analyzers
without the parameter, so shipped behavior is untouched by construction.

**Pre-declared expectations for the first sweep** (unexplainedToneCost at 2.0
baseline, then 1.5, 1.0, 0.75, 0.5, on arm BC-modal and A0, development split):

- The added-tone family's exact share rises as the price falls (base readings
  begin outcompeting absorbed supersets); BC-modal exact should rise if the
  lever works, with the melody-absorption samples (Dm versus Dm(maj7)) flipping
  first.
- The dense census consistency (baseline 0.9755 pop, 1.0000 classical) must not
  degrade materially; dense sets are where cheap unexplained tones could
  fragment naming.
- The standing rows are re-read at every price point; flips get exposure mass
  attached under review-on-flip.
- Solo goldens, comping suite, and the benchmark check stay green at any price
  point proposed for adoption; blast radius via pool_diff with exposure
  weighting.
- Expected failure mode, stated up front: too-cheap unexplained tones make the
  engine lazy (naming subsets everywhere, eating legitimate extended
  vocabulary); the sweep exists to find whether any price between 2.0 and 0.5
  beats the baseline before that laziness dominates.

**Plain-English reading.** We now have a safe way to try out different prices
for unexplained notes without touching what the app ships: a research-only dial,
defaulting to today's behavior. Scouting also found why the shell idea is harder
than a price change: the engine flatly refuses to consider a power chord that
leaves any note unexplained, and it has no way to write a seventh as a color on
one. Both walls are now on the map before we spend effort walking into them.
